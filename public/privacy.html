WRENCHED

PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Wrenched ("we," "us," "our") provides a car maintenance tracking and vehicle information app available on the web at wrenched.shop and as an app on iOS and Android. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how it's handled.

INFORMATION YOU PROVIDE

Account information. When you sign up, we collect your email address and a password to create your account. We use this to let you log in and to send you account-related messages (like a password reset).

Vehicle and maintenance data. Information you enter about your cars — year, make, model, mileage, maintenance history, and any modifications you log — is stored so you can access it across sessions. This data is yours; we don't use it for anything beyond providing the app's features to you.

INFORMATION COLLECTED AUTOMATICALLY

Location. If you use the "Find a Shop" feature, we ask for your device's location to show nearby repair shops, dealers, and service chains. Location is only requested when you actively use this feature — we don't track your location in the background, and you can decline the permission and use the rest of the app normally.

PAYMENT INFORMATION

When you purchase Wrenched, payment is handled directly by Stripe (on the web) or by Apple/Google (in the iOS and Android apps) — we never see or store your full card number. We do receive confirmation that a purchase was completed, which we use to unlock the app for your account.

THIRD-PARTY SERVICES WE USE

  • Supabase — hosts our database and handles account authentication
  • Stripe — processes web payments
  • RevenueCat — manages in-app purchases on iOS and Android, working with Apple and Google's own payment systems
  • Google Places API — powers the "Find a Shop" feature's search for nearby repair shops

Each of these services has its own privacy practices governing the data they process on our behalf.

WHAT WE DON'T DO

We don't sell your personal information. We don't show ads or share your data with advertisers.

DATA RETENTION AND DELETION

We retain your account and vehicle data for as long as your account is active. If you'd like your account and associated data deleted, contact us at MattJustice1999@gmail.com and we'll process the request.

CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Wrenched is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

CONTACT US

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach us at MattJustice1999@gmail.com.
