COMMON QUESTIONS

How do I cancel or manage my purchase?
Wrenched is a one-time purchase, not a subscription — there's nothing to cancel. If you're still in your free trial and haven't purchased yet, no charge will ever occur unless you choose to unlock the app.

I bought Wrenched on one device — can I use it on another?
Yes. Use "Restore Purchase" on the paywall screen, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you originally purchased with.

A torque spec or vehicle detail looks wrong.
Email the make/model/year and what you're seeing — vehicle data is compiled from public sources and corrections are always welcome.

How do I delete my account and data?
Email the address above and I'll process the request.
